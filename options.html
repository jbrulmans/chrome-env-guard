Display

Reserve space pads the page by the width of the frame, so the marker sits beside your content instead of on top of it. A site's own fixed headers can still slide under the frame, and pages built on 100vh may gain a small scrollbar.

A site's own fixed menu bar is positioned against the viewport, so nothing can push it down. If your app has one, keep the frame on sides and bottom and the bar at the bottom — then the top of the page is never touched.

Tab title prefix — {label} is replaced with the environment name, and any trailing space is kept. Leave empty for no prefix.
